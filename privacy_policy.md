# Privacy policy

This discord app/bot collects the following information from your use of the `/connect` command, in order to enable its functionality:

- Your numeric discord ID
- Your cryptohack username

We do not use this data for any reasons beyond the functionality of the bot, and do not share this information with anyone outside the normal functionality of the bot.
That is, people querying the bot can discover your cryptohack username, given your discord identity.

If you wish for this data to be removed, issue the `/disconnect` command with the bot.
